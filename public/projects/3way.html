Threeway Showdown

November 2025 Events Coordination and Marketing BMEUSA

This was a threeway showdown party, planned by the social coordinators of three engineering student associations: Biomedical, Environmental and Integrated Engineering. The country themed event was held in the Engineering Student Centre and featured a DJ, line dancing and custom mixed beverages. This was a large success, with over 200 attendees. Highlights included the marketing campaign and commitment to the theme! This event was made possible in compliance with the Government of BC and UBC's alcoholic event planning policies and guidelines.
