nice to meet you!

I’m Sophia, a fourth-year biomedical engineering student at the University of British Columbia, specializing in bioinformatics and minoring in commerce through the Sauder School of Business. My interests sit at the intersection of software development, data engineering, and biotech.

My connection to medicine and health started with my own childhood health complications. I chose engineering because I wanted to build things that could reach more patients than I could ever meet personally. That motivation continues to shape the problems I’m drawn to.

I’m finishing an eight-month Asset Management Co-op with UBC Municipal this December. Working alongside people with different perspectives and technical backgrounds has been a highlight of the experience. Looking ahead, I’m excited to take ownership of technically challenging projects and contribute to a fast-moving team in software, data, or biotech.

Beyond technical work, I love bringing people together. As BMEUSA’s VP Social, I helped deliver one of our most successful social years, organizing everything from small gatherings to engineering parties and formals. Now, as VP Academic, I collaborate with faculty and advocate for students.

Outside engineering, you’ll find me exploring fashion, hosting friends, heading to pilates, or planning my next good meal. I also love travelling—discovering new places, trying local food, and finding inspiration along the way. Ask me for a restaurant recommendation!

Explore my projects or wander into the personal corner of this site to get to know me better. I’m always happy to connect, exchange ideas, or hear about an interesting opportunity.
